Calculator gives you exact answers, not rounded guesses.

Behind the clean keypad is the exact arithmetic of Android's ExactCalculator: numbers like √2, π and e are kept exact while you work, so 1 ÷ 3 × 3 is exactly 1 and every digit it shows is correct. Swipe the answer to scroll through as many digits as you want.

Everything you need
• Live result as you type
• √, π, e, powers, factorials and percentages
• sin, cos, tan and their inverses, ln and log
• Degrees or radians
• Answers as fractions, or with their leading digits
• Memory, copy and paste
• History of your calculations — pull down the display to see it

Built for every screen
• Portrait and landscape, with the scientific keys always at hand in landscape
• Layouts for phones, tablets and foldables
• Light and dark themes

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• No internet permission: your calculations never leave your phone
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/PvotCalculator
